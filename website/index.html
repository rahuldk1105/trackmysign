Sign PDFs with your DSC.
Without Adobe.

SignDesk is a small desktop app for Windows and macOS that signs PDFs with your USB DSC token. Open a PDF, place the signature, enter your PIN. The signature validates in Adobe Reader and earlier signatures stay intact.

Features

Everything a director needs to sign. Nothing else.

Built for the documents auditors and company secretaries send every week: financial statements, board resolutions, MCA attachments.

Your file stays untouched

The signature is appended as an incremental update. Every byte of the original PDF is preserved, so nothing in the document can shift or break.

Co-signing that works

PDFs already signed by your CA, auditor or a co-director keep their signatures valid. Yours is added alongside them.

Click to place

Click anywhere on any page, drag to move, drag the corner to resize. The preview matches the final stamp exactly, and the position is remembered.

Talks to your token directly

Uses the token's own PKCS#11 driver. The private key never leaves the token; SignDesk only asks it to sign a hash.

PIN is never stored

Entered for one signature and discarded. SignDesk warns you when the token reports few attempts left, before it locks.

Light, fast, native

A <3 MB app built with Rust and Tauri. No Java, no browser plug-ins, no background services. Light and dark themes included.

How it works

Three steps. About ten seconds.

  1. 01

    Open the PDF

    Drag it into the window or choose it. Your token is detected automatically.

  2. 02

    Place the signature

    Click where it should go. Set the reason and location once; they're remembered.

  3. 03

    Enter your PIN

    The signed copy is saved next to the original as name_signed.pdf.

FAQ

Questions

Is a signature from SignDesk legally valid?

The legal weight comes from your DSC, issued by a licensed Certifying Authority under the IT Act, 2000 — not from the software that applies it. SignDesk produces a standard PKCS#7 signature with your certificate, which Adobe Reader and other validators verify the same way as any other DSC signature.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. SignDesk works entirely on your computer. There is no server, no account and no analytics. You can run it with the network disconnected.

Will it break signatures that are already on the PDF?

No. SignDesk writes an incremental update: it appends the new signature after the existing content instead of rewriting the file, which is exactly what keeps earlier signatures valid.

Which DSC tokens work?

Tested with Hypersecu HyperPKI HYP2003. Tokens from the same driver family (Feitian ePass2003) are expected to work. Support for automatically detecting other PKCS#11 tokens is planned — open an issue with your token's name and driver and it can be added.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when installing?

The installers are not yet signed with a paid code-signing certificate, so the OS doesn't recognise the publisher. The code is open, and every release is built publicly by GitHub Actions from the source in the repository.

Does it work for MCA e-forms?

SignDesk signs regular PDFs such as financial statements, resolutions and attachments. MCA's own web-based forms are signed through the MCA portal's signer utility, not with a desktop app.

Is it really free?

Yes. SignDesk is MIT-licensed open source. Use it, change it, ship it with your own products.
